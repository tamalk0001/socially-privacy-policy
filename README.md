# Sociallly Privacy Policy

**Effective date and last updated:** November 10, 2026

Sociallly is a Windows desktop application for managing content, scheduling and publishing to Facebook Pages, and reviewing publishing results. This policy covers the desktop app, its optional browser extensions, and this policy page. Sociallly is operated by **Tamal Krishna Chandra**. For privacy questions or requests, email [tamalkchandra@gmail.com](mailto:tamalkchandra@gmail.com).

## Information Sociallly processes

- **Facebook Page connections and activity.** When you connect Facebook, Sociallly uses the access tokens and permissions you provide to identify the Pages available to you and perform the actions you authorize. Depending on the feature and permissions, it processes Page names, IDs and profile images, access tokens, post and media IDs, publication status and timestamps, and available metrics such as views, engagement, follower counts, and comment totals.
- **Content and workspace information.** Sociallly processes the drafts, captions, hashtags, images, videos, notes, Page knowledge, documents, schedules, and settings you add or create. It also stores publishing history and errors so you can review results and resolve issues.
- **Optional browser extensions.** Group Poster and Group Collector interact with Facebook in the browser when you use those features. They can process Page or group names, IDs and URLs, available membership or posting status, selected posts, and action outcomes. Group Poster exchanges queue and status information with the desktop app through a connection on your computer. Facebook separately handles your browser session and actions on its site.
- **Communications.** If you email the operator, your email address, message, and any information you include are used to respond and handle your request.

## How information is used and shared

- **Meta.** Sociallly sends credentials and requests to Meta to connect Pages, check permissions and publication status, retrieve available insights, and schedule or publish the content you choose. Publishing or sharing can make your media and captions visible to the audience set by Facebook. Meta handles that information under its own policies.
- **Optional AI and document features.** When you use AI generation, analysis, or document features, relevant prompts, text, document content, or media may be sent to the configured provider, such as OpenAI, Kie AI, or a custom endpoint you select. The provider receives the information necessary to complete the request and handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. Use these features only with content you are authorized to share.
- **Other websites.** When you use the public video downloader, Sociallly contacts the video platform for the link you provide. When you visit this policy page, GitHub hosts the page and may process ordinary website access data under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Legal obligations.** The operator may disclose information when required by applicable law. The operator does not sell Meta Platform Data or use it for advertising profiles.

## Storage, security, and retention

Sociallly stores workspace records and media in the project folder you select. Saved Facebook access tokens and provider credentials are stored separately on the computer using Windows-backed encryption. Workspace files and exported backups can contain private content and should be protected with your device and storage access controls.

Local drafts, records, and media may remain until you delete them or an applicable app cleanup rule removes them. Media shared with another local record can remain while that record needs it. Backup copies you export remain wherever you save them until you remove them. Meta and any other services you choose may retain information according to their own policies. Privacy correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations.

## Your choices and data deletion

- **Stop future Facebook access.** Disable publishing and revoke the app’s access through Facebook’s app or business integration settings. You can also remove saved credentials and unpair the browser extension where applicable.
- **Delete local data.** Use Sociallly’s local deletion controls for selected records. If you want to remove a workspace entirely, also remove the applicable local project files and backup copies you control. Uninstalling the app or revoking access does not by itself erase those files.
- **Manage content already on Facebook.** Deleting a local record does not remove a post or cancel a schedule already sent to Facebook. Manage those items separately on Facebook or in Meta Business Suite.
- **Request access, correction, or deletion.** Email [tamalkchandra@gmail.com](mailto:tamalkchandra@gmail.com) with the subject **Sociallly privacy request**. Describe the request and identify the affected Page or workspace if relevant. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or API keys. The operator may ask for reasonable verification and will address requests for information under the operator’s control in accordance with applicable law. The operator can also provide instructions for deleting data stored on your own computer.
